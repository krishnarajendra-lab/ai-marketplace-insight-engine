# AI Marketplace Insight Engine

## Overview

AI Marketplace Insight Engine is an AI-powered market research workflow built using n8n and Google Gemini.

The system helps entrepreneurs, e-commerce sellers, manufacturers, and marketers quickly evaluate product opportunities by generating marketplace intelligence from a simple product category input.

---

## Problem Statement

Market research is often time-consuming and requires manual analysis of customer feedback, competitor offerings, pricing strategies, and market trends.

Many small businesses and independent sellers lack the resources to perform comprehensive product research before launching or improving products.

---

## Solution

This workflow automates marketplace analysis using AI.

The user enters a product category and executes the workflow. The AI then generates actionable business insights including:

* Customer complaints
* Positive customer sentiment
* Market gaps
* Product improvement opportunities
* Pricing opportunities
* Competitive insights

---

## Technologies Used

* n8n
* Google Gemini
* AI Prompt Engineering
* Workflow Automation

---

## Workflow

Set Product Category → Basic LLM Chain → Google Gemini → Marketplace Insights

---

## Demo Categories

The workflow was demonstrated using:

* Copper Water Bottles
* Yoga Mats

The same workflow can be adapted to analyze virtually any product category.

---

## How To Use

1. Open the workflow in n8n.
2. Navigate to the Set Node.
3. Change the value of product_category.
4. Click Execute Workflow.
5. Review the generated marketplace intelligence report.
